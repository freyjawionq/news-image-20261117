  The panel sub-line now reads "UK SANCTIONS SETTLEMENT FIRMS AS

  "Russian" and placed them "north of Fukuoka". Per Japan's Defense
  Ministry (Kyodo), they were suspected Russian aircraft northwest
  of Okinoshima Island, Fukuoka Prefecture; the panel now says so.
